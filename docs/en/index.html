J1 · Tests

Everything that can break, checked

Every test ends with a verdict: works fine, has issues or doesn't work. Automatic tests suggest one and you decide.

  • U01

    Summary

    Make, model and serial, CPU, RAM per slot, GPUs, drives, battery, motherboard and BIOS.

  • U02

    Full check-up

    Walks you through the hands-on tests while it runs the automatic ones by itself. About 20 minutes.

  • U03

    Keyboard

    Keyboard drawn with its real shape (ISO, ANSI, ABNT2). Every key that arrives lights up.

  • U04

    Touchpad and mouse

    An area that fills in as you move, buttons, gestures, wheel, touchscreen and pen.

  • U05

    Display

    Full-screen colours, gradients and grid to spot dead pixels and backlight bleed.

  • U06

    Sound

    Left and right speaker, a low-to-high sweep and the microphone with record and play back.

  • U07

    Camera

    Live picture from every camera. Warns if it is black. Nothing is saved.

  • U08

    Network

    Adapters, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, step-by-step connection check and Internet speed.

  • U09

    USB ports

    Marks every port that responds with its real speed. Spots USB 3 running as USB 2.

  • U10

    Battery and charger

    Health and cycles, live power draw, real runtime per type of use and a charger test.

  • U11

    Drives

    SMART health, errors logged by the system and real read and write speed.

  • U12

    RAM

    8 patterns over all free memory, bit by bit.

  • U13

    CPU

    Stress test that detects overheating, thermal throttling and calculation errors.

  • U14

    GPU

    Stress any GPU with verified calculations, plus a video memory test.

  • U15

    Sleep

    Sleeps, wakes up and checks that the network and devices come back.

  • U16

    Motherboard

    Firmware, TPM, failing devices, PCIe links, hardware errors and CMOS battery.

  • J2

    PDF report

    Every test with measurements, notes and verdicts, on an A4 page ready to hand over.

J4 · How to use it

Two ways to use it

Check the whole machine with the step-by-step guide, or go straight to the part you care about. You can mix both: whatever you test on your own also goes into the report.

Whole machine · about 20 min

Guided check-up

To check a machine from top to bottom before selling, returning or buying it.

  1. In the sidebar, open Full check-up and press Start the check-up.
  2. Do what each step asks: press keys, look at the screen, listen, plug in a USB device… Meanwhile, drives, network, memory, CPU and GPU are tested on their own.
  3. Skip any step you don't need. When you are done, press Finish and view the report.

One part · at your pace

Test by test

To check something specific: a faulty keyboard, a suspicious battery, a noisy drive.

  1. Pick the test in the sidebar, for example Keyboard or Battery.
  2. Follow the on-screen instructions. Automatic tests suggest a verdict.
  3. At the bottom, mark Works fine, Has issues or Doesn't work. The dot in the sidebar changes colour.

Either way, the report gathers everything you tested: add your notes and press Save report as PDF.

J5 · Specs

Specs

Made by technicians, for technicians. One file, nothing to install.

Your machines and your customers' stay on the bench

Bancada collects and sends no data. Camera and microphone are never recorded, and the report stays in your Documents folder. It only goes online to:

  • Check the connection (router, DNS and a secure website).
  • Measure speed, only when you press the button (Cloudflare).
  • Compare the motherboard clock with Internet time.
  • Open web pages in the browsing mode of the battery test.
